# Layer 2.05.25 — Schedule & Business Hours

## Purpose

Control flow behaviour according to operational schedules.

## Classification

**Required**

## Scope

- Open
- Closed
- Extended hours
- Regional hours
- Time zones
- Emergency schedules

## Activities

- Inventory schedules.
- Define schedule groups.
- Define time zones.
- Configure schedules.
- Map flows.
- Test open.
- Test closed.
- Test boundary conditions.

## Acceptance Criteria

Flows follow the approved operational schedule.

## Definition of Done

Business-hours behaviour is configured and tested.
