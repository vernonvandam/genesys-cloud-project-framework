# Layer 2.03.27 — Fax & Special Telephony Services

## 1. Purpose

Identify and manage special telephony requirements that do not follow standard agent voice patterns.

## 2. Classification

**Conditional**

## 3. Discovery

Identify:

- Fax
- Modem
- Alarm lines
- Door phones
- Paging
- Analogue devices
- Conference devices
- Special service numbers
- Legacy systems

## 4. Activities

- Inventory special services.
- Determine business criticality.
- Determine Genesys Cloud compatibility.
- Design replacement/integration approach.
- Test special services.
- Document limitations.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

All special telephony services have an approved target-state solution.

## 6. Definition of Done

Special telephony services are migrated, integrated or explicitly retained with documented ownership.
