# Layer 2.13.10 — Privacy & Compliance

## Capability Definition

Privacy & Compliance ensures migration activities comply with privacy legislation, contractual obligations, regulatory requirements and customer policies.

---

# 1. Scope

- Privacy
- PII
- Sensitive data
- Data minimisation
- Data residency
- Data transfer
- Data retention
- Access controls
- Auditability

# 2. Classification

**Required**

- Identify cross-border transfers.
- Identify customer policies.
- Identify masking requirements.

# 4. Design Activities
