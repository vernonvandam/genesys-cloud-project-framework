# Layer 2.13.43 — Transition Management

## Capability Definition

Transition Management coordinates the movement of users, business processes, support responsibilities and operational ownership from the legacy platform to Genesys Cloud.

---

# 1. Scope

- User transition
- Business transition
- Operational transition
- Support transition
- Communications
- Training
- Legacy transition

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

Identify transition groups, impacts, dependencies and readiness requirements.

---

# 4. Design Activities

Define transition waves, communications, training and ownership transfer.

---

- Handover

Provide transition owners and operational teams.

---

Validate users and support teams are ready for transition.

---

- Transition Plan
- Communications Plan
- Transition Readiness Report

User population and organisational complexity.

---

Target users and support teams are successfully transitioned.

---

# 14. Definition of Done

Transition is complete and operational ownership is established.
