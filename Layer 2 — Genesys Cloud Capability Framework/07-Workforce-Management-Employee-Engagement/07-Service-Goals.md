# Layer 2.07.07 — Service Goals

## Purpose

Define the operational service targets used to calculate workforce requirements.

## Classification

**Required**

## Potential Measures

- Service level
- Average speed of answer
- Abandonment
- Response time
- Occupancy
- Utilisation
- Queue performance

## Activities

- Identify business targets.
- Identify regulatory requirements.
- Define service-level target.
- Define response target.
- Define abandonment target.
- Define occupancy assumptions.
- Define operating hours.
- Configure service goals.
- Validate calculations.

## Acceptance Criteria

Service goals represent approved business requirements.

## Definition of Done

Service goals are approved and usable by forecasting.
