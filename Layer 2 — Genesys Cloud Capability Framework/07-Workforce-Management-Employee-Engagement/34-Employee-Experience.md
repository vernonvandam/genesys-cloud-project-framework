# Layer 2.07.34 — Employee Experience

## Purpose

Ensure WFM processes support both operational performance and employee experience.

## Classification

**Required**

## Considerations

- Schedule predictability
- Schedule flexibility
- Time-off access
- Shift preferences
- Fairness
- Workload
- Break management
- Self-service
- Notifications
- Communication

## Activities

- Define employee experience requirements.
- Gather employee feedback.
- Identify friction points.
- Define experience KPIs.
- Validate schedule model.
- Validate self-service.
- Validate communications.
- Establish improvement process.

## Acceptance Criteria

Employee experience is explicitly considered in WFM design.

## Definition of Done

Employee experience requirements are incorporated into the WFM operating model.
