# Layer 2.07.30 — Employee Engagement

## Purpose

## Classification

**Conditional**

## Activities

- Identify employee feedback mechanisms.

Employee engagement has defined objectives, measurements and ownership.

## Definition of Done

Employee engagement model is established.
