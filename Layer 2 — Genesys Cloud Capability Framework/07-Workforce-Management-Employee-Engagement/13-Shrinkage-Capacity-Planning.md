# Layer 2.07.13 — Shrinkage & Capacity Planning

## Purpose

Account for workforce time that is not available for customer interactions.

## Potential Shrinkage

- Annual leave
- Sick leave
- Training
- Meetings
- Coaching
- Breaks
- Administrative work
- System downtime
- Other planned activities

## Activities

- Inventory shrinkage categories.
- Determine historical shrinkage.
- Define target assumptions.
- Configure shrinkage.
- Validate capacity calculations.
- Review staffing impact.

## Acceptance Criteria

Staffing calculations account for approved shrinkage assumptions.

## Definition of Done

Shrinkage model is approved.
