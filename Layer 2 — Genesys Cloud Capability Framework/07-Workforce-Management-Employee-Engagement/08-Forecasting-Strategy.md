# Layer 2.07.08 — Forecasting Strategy

## Purpose

Define the methodology used to forecast customer demand and workforce requirements.

## Classification

**Required**

## Activities

- Identify historical periods.
- Identify seasonal patterns.
- Identify growth assumptions.
- Identify campaign effects.
- Identify anomalies.
- Define forecast horizon.
- Define forecast granularity.
- Define review process.
- Define forecast accuracy KPI.

## Deliverables

- Forecast strategy
- Forecast assumptions
- Forecast governance

## Acceptance Criteria

Forecast methodology is approved by business and WFM stakeholders.

## Definition of Done

Forecast strategy is ready for implementation.
