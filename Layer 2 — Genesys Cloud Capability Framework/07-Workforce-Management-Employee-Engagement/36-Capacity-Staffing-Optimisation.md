# Layer 2.07.36 — Capacity & Staffing Optimisation

## Purpose

Continuously optimise workforce capacity against customer demand.

## Activities

- Compare forecast vs actual.
- Compare staffing vs requirement.
- Identify recurring gaps.
- Review shrinkage.
- Review occupancy.
- Review schedule efficiency.
- Identify hiring requirements.
- Identify overtime requirements.
- Identify cross-training opportunities.
- Update capacity model.

## Acceptance Criteria

Workforce capacity decisions are supported by measurable data.

## Definition of Done

Capacity optimisation is part of BAU planning.
