# Layer 2.14.28 — Quality & Recording Operations

## Capability Definition

Quality & Recording Operations defines the BAU processes for managing interaction recording, quality management and associated operational controls.

---

# 1. Scope

- Recording
- Recording policies
- Quality management
- Evaluation forms
- Quality programmes
- Recording access
- Retention
