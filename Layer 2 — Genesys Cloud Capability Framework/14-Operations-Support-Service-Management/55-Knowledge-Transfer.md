# Layer 2.14.55 — Knowledge Transfer

## Capability Definition

Knowledge Transfer ensures project knowledge is deliberately transferred from implementation teams to customer operational and technical teams.

---

# 1. Scope

- Technical knowledge
- Operational knowledge
- Configuration knowledge
- Support knowledge
- Architecture
- Troubleshooting
- Handover sessions
- Knowledge validation

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify knowledge recipients.
- Identify knowledge gaps.
- Identify critical capabilities.
- Identify required sessions.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define knowledge-transfer plan.
- Define session structure.
- Define competency criteria.
- Define evidence of transfer.
- Define follow-up support.

---

- Training

# 8. Roles

- Operations Lead
- Technical SMEs

---

# 9. Customer Responsibilities

- Provide participants.
- Attend knowledge-transfer sessions.
- Demonstrate competency.
- Sign off transfer.

---

# 10. Testing

Validate participants can perform agreed operational tasks.

---

# 11. Deliverables

- Knowledge Transfer Plan
- Session Materials
- Knowledge Transfer Records
- Sign-off

---

# 12. Effort Drivers

Capability breadth, recipient population and technical complexity.

---

# 13. Acceptance Criteria

- Sessions delivered.
- Knowledge validated.
- Gaps closed.
- Sign-off obtained.

---

# 14. Definition of Done

Required project knowledge has been transferred and accepted by the BAU team.
