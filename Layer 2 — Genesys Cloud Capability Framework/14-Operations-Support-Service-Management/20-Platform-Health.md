# Layer 2.14.20 — Platform Health

## Capability Definition

Platform Health provides operational oversight of the health, availability and service condition of the Genesys Cloud platform and its critical dependencies.

---

# 1. Scope

- Genesys Cloud service health
- Platform status
- Availability
- Service incidents
- Health monitoring
- Operational impact
- Vendor notifications

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify critical platform services.
- Identify customer health requirements.
- Identify Genesys service status monitoring.
- Identify operational health indicators.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define platform health model.
- Define health checks.
- Define service impact assessment.
- Define escalation.
- Define health reporting.
