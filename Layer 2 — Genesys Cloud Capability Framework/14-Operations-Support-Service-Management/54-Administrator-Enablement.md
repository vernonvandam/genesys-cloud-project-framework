# Layer 2.14.54 — Administrator Enablement

## Capability Definition

Administrator Enablement prepares customer administrators to safely manage Genesys Cloud configuration, users, routing, Architect, reporting and other assigned capabilities.

---

# 1. Scope

- Platform administration
- User management
- Roles
- Routing
- Architect
- Telephony
- Reporting
- Integrations
- Troubleshooting
- Governance

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

- Identify responsibilities.
- Identify required permissions.
- Identify skill gaps.
- Identify training requirements.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define hands-on exercises.
- Define administrative boundaries.
