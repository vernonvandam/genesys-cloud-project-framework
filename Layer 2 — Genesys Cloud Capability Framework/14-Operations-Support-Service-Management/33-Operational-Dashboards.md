# Layer 2.14.33 — Operational Dashboards

## Capability Definition

Operational Dashboards provide real-time or near-real-time visibility into Genesys Cloud service performance and operational health.

---

# 1. Scope

- Operational dashboards
- Queue metrics
- Agent metrics
- Service metrics
- Platform health
- Integrations
- Alerts
- KPI visualisation
