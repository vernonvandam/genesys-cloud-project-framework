# Layer 2.14.39 — Availability Management

## Capability Definition

Availability Management ensures Genesys Cloud services meet agreed availability objectives and that service-impacting availability risks are actively managed.

- Service availability
- Monitoring
- Outages
- Availability reporting
- Service dependencies
- Availability improvement
