# Layer 2.14.02 — Service Ownership

## Capability Definition

Service Ownership defines accountability for Genesys Cloud services, capabilities, configuration domains and operational outcomes.

---

# 1. Scope

- Service ownership
- Capability ownership
- Technical ownership
- Business ownership
- Configuration ownership
- Operational ownership
- Escalation ownership
- RACI

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify existing service owners.
- Identify business owners.
- Identify technical owners.
- Identify platform administrators.
- Identify integration owners.
- Identify vendor ownership boundaries.
- Identify unresolved ownership gaps.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define service ownership model.
- Define business and technical ownership.
- Define accountability boundaries.
- Define escalation ownership.
- Define RACI.
- Define ownership for critical capabilities.
