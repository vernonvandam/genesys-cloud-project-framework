# Layer 2.14.23 — Digital Operations

## Capability Definition

Digital Operations establishes BAU procedures for managing Genesys Cloud digital channels and associated routing, messaging and customer interaction capabilities.

---

# 1. Scope

- Digital channels
- Messaging
- Web messaging
- Email
- Social/messaging channels where applicable
- Digital routing
- Digital queues
- Digital troubleshooting
- Digital integrations

---

# 2. Classification

**Required where digital is deployed**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify digital channels.
- Identify digital owners.
- Identify channel dependencies.
- Identify operational issues.
- Identify monitoring requirements.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define digital operating procedures.
- Define channel support boundaries.
- Define digital troubleshooting.
- Define integration escalation.
- Define monitoring requirements.
