# Layer 2.09.24 — Quality Management Reporting

## Purpose

Measure interaction quality and quality programme performance.

## Classification

**Conditional**

## Metrics

- Evaluations completed
- Evaluation scores
- Critical failures
- Quality trends
- Evaluator activity
- Coaching outcomes

## Activities

- Define quality metrics.
- Configure reporting.
- Validate.
- Secure access.
- Test.

## Definition of Done

Quality reporting is operational.
