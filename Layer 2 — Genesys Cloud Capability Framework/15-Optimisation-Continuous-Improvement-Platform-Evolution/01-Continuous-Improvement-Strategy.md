# Layer 2.15.01 — Continuous Improvement Strategy

## Capability Definition

Defines the strategic framework for continuously improving Genesys Cloud capabilities, operational outcomes, customer experience, employee experience, technology adoption and business value.

---

# 1. Scope

- Continuous improvement strategy
- Improvement objectives
- Improvement governance
- Performance baselines
- Improvement opportunities
- Benefits realisation
- Improvement roadmap
- Strategic alignment

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Review current-state performance.
- Identify strategic business objectives.
- Review customer and employee experience.
- Identify operational pain points.
- Review existing improvement initiatives.
- Identify improvement governance requirements.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define improvement strategy.
- Define improvement objectives.
- Define improvement principles.
- Define governance model.
- Define improvement lifecycle.
- Define benefits measurement.
- Define strategic roadmap.

- Governance established.
