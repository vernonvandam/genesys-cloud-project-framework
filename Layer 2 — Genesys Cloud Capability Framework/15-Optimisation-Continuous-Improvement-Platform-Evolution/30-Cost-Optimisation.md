# Layer 2.15.30 — Cost Optimisation

## Capability Definition

Identifies opportunities to reduce or control Genesys Cloud total cost of ownership while preserving service quality, compliance and business outcomes.

---

# 1. Scope

- Licensing
- Telephony
- Infrastructure
- Integrations
- Automation
- Operational effort
- Cost governance

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Establish cost baseline.
- Review licensing.
- Review telephony costs.
- Review operational effort.
- Identify unnecessary consumption.
- Identify cost drivers.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define cost optimisation objectives.
- Identify cost reduction opportunities.
- Assess business impact.
- Define cost governance.
- Define monitoring.
