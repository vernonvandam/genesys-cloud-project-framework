# Layer 2.15.34 — Feature Adoption

## Capability Definition

Identifies, assesses and drives adoption of relevant Genesys Cloud capabilities that provide measurable business or operational value.

---

# 1. Scope

- Feature discovery
- Capability assessment
- Adoption planning
- Business cases
- Pilot
- Training
- Adoption measurement

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Review current feature usage.
- Identify unused capabilities.
- Review business pain points.
- Identify potential feature opportunities.
- Assess readiness.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define adoption criteria.
- Define business case.
- Define pilot approach.
- Define adoption measures.
- Define training requirements.
- Define rollout strategy.
