# 31 — User Acceptance Testing

## Capability Definition

Provides formal business validation that the Genesys Cloud solution meets approved business requirements and is acceptable for production.

---

# 1. Scope

- Business workflows
- Customer journeys
- Agent workflows
- Supervisor workflows
- Reports
- Integrations
- Acceptance

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify UAT participants.
- Identify business processes.
- Identify acceptance criteria.
- Identify UAT environment.
- Identify UAT schedule.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define UAT scenarios.
- Define business test cases.
- Define UAT entry criteria.
- Define UAT exit criteria.
- Define sign-off process.

---

# 5. Implementation Activities

```text
Prepare UAT environment
Prepare UAT data
Brief business testers
Execute UAT scenarios
Record results
Record defects
Retest defects
Execute regression
Review outstanding issues
Prepare UAT report
Obtain business sign-off
```

---

# 6. Dependencies

- Test Planning
- Test Data
- End-to-End Testing
- Defect Management
- Regression Testing

---

# 7. Layer 1 Mapping

Primary:

- Phase 8 — Testing & Validation
- Phase 9 — Operational Readiness & Cutover Preparation

---

# 8. Roles

- UAT Lead
- Business SMEs
- Test Lead
- Business Owner
- Project Manager

---

# 9. Customer Responsibilities

- Provide UAT participants.
- Execute UAT.
- Prioritise defects.
- Approve UAT.
- Provide formal sign-off.

---

# 10. Testing

UAT must be executed against approved business scenarios and acceptance criteria.

---

# 11. Deliverables

- UAT Plan
- UAT Test Cases
- UAT Results
- UAT Defect Register
- UAT Sign-Off

---

# 12. Effort Drivers

- Number of business processes
- Number of testers
- Number of scenarios
- UAT duration
- Defect volume

---

# 13. Acceptance Criteria

- UAT executed.
- Critical defects resolved.
- Business acceptance achieved.
- Sign-off obtained.

---

# 14. Definition of Done

Formal business acceptance has been obtained.
