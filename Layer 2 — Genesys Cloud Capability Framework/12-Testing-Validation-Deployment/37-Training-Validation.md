# 37 — Training Validation

## Capability Definition

Validates that end-user, administrator, supervisor and support training is complete and enables users to perform their required responsibilities.

---

# 1. Scope

- Agent training
- Supervisor training
- Administrator training
- Support training
- WFM training
- Quality training
- Reporting training

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify personas.
- Identify training requirements.
- Identify training materials.
- Identify training environments.
- Identify competency requirements.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define training validation scenarios.
- Define competency criteria.
- Define attendance requirements.
- Define training acceptance.

---

# 5. Implementation Activities

```text
Review training materials
Validate training environment
Deliver training
Record attendance
Execute competency scenarios
Collect feedback
Identify knowledge gaps
Provide remediation
Retest competency
Confirm readiness
```

# 10. Testing

---

# 11. Deliverables

# 12. Effort Drivers

- Number of users

---

# 13. Acceptance Criteria

- Required users trained.
- Competency demonstrated.
- Gaps addressed.

---

# 14. Definition of Done

Required users are trained and validated as production-ready.
