# Layer 2.01.05 — Business Units

## Capability Definition

Business units are used where applicable to support workforce-management organisational structures.

---

# 1. Scope

- Business units
- Business-unit ownership
- WFM relationship
- Organisational mapping
- Forecasting implications

---

# 2. Classification

**Conditional**

Primarily applicable when Workforce Management capabilities are included.

---

# 3. Discovery

Determine:

- Is WFM in scope?
- How many operational business units exist?
- How are agents grouped?
- What are the planning boundaries?
- What service levels apply?
- What forecasting structures are required?

---

# 4. Design

- Define business units.
- Define naming.
- Map agents.
- Map management units where applicable.
- Define planning boundaries.
- Define WFM ownership.

---

# 5. Implementation

```text
Confirm WFM scope
Define business-unit model
Create business units
Map organisational structure
Validate WFM relationships
Document configuration
```

# 6. Layer 1 Mapping

- Phase 6 — Solution Build

- Phase 8 — Testing

# 7. Roles

- WFM Specialist
- Genesys Cloud Architect
- Genesys Cloud Engineer

---

# 8. Acceptance Criteria

Business units support the approved WFM organisational model and have passed validation.

---

# 9. Definition of Done

Business units are configured and accepted where WFM requires them.
