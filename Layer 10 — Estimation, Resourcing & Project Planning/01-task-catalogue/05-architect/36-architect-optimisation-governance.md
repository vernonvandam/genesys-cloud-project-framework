## Capability Objective

Establish ongoing Architect governance, optimisation and continuous-improvement processes.

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Establish Architect optimisation metrics.
2. Review flow performance and complexity.
3. Identify improvement opportunities.
4. Establish ongoing governance.
