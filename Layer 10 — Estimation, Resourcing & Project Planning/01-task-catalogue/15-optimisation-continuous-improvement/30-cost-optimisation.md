| Attribute | Value |

## Capability Objective

Identify opportunities to reduce total cost of ownership while preserving business value, service quality, security and compliance.

## Layer 1 Mapping

| Phase | Application |
|---|---|
| P02 | Establish cost baseline |
| P03 | Identify cost reduction opportunities |
| P04 | Assess solution impacts |
| P09 | Implement and monitor savings |
| P12 | Establish ongoing cost governance |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Establish cost baseline.
2. Identify cost drivers.
3. Identify cost optimisation opportunities.
4. Assess business and technical impact.
5. Track savings.
