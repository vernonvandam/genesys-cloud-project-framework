| Attribute | Value |

## Capability Objective

Define and maintain the target evolution of the Genesys Cloud platform, capabilities, integrations, architecture, operating model and business outcomes.

## Layer 1 Mapping

| Phase | Application |
|---|---|
| P02 | Assess current platform state |
| P03 | Define roadmap objectives |
| P04 | Define target-state evolution |
| P09 | Maintain BAU roadmap |
| P12 | Establish ongoing roadmap ownership |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Assess current platform state.
2. Identify future business requirements.
3. Identify platform evolution opportunities.
4. Define roadmap initiatives.
5. Prioritise roadmap outcomes.
6. Establish roadmap governance.
