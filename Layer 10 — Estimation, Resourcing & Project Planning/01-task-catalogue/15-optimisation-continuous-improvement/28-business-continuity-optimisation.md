| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|

## Capability Objective

Continuously improve the ability of the contact centre to maintain critical customer services during disruption.

## Layer 1 Mapping

| Phase | Application |
|---|---|
| P02 | Assess continuity posture |
| P03 | Define continuity requirements |
| P04 | Design continuity improvements |
| P08 | Test continuity |
| P09 | Operationalise continuity |
| P11 | Validate production readiness |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Review continuity requirements.
2. Assess current continuity controls.
3. Identify gaps.
4. Improve continuity arrangements.
5. Test and validate.
