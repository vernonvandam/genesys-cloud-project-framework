Optimise interaction recording coverage, storage, retention, access, security, retrieval, compliance and operational performance.

| Phase | Application |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Assess recording configuration.
2. Review recording coverage and retention.
3. Identify security and cost opportunities.
4. Implement improvements.
5. Validate recording outcomes.
