## Capability Objective

Ensure WFM processes provide an effective employee experience while maintaining operational requirements.

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Assess employee experience.
2. Define employee journeys.
3. Identify friction points.
4. Validate user experience.
5. Establish ongoing feedback.
