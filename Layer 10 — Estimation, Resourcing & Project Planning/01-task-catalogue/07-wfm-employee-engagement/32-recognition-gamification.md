| Task Catalogue ID | 07.32 |

## Capability Objective

Implement recognition and gamification capabilities where they support approved employee engagement objectives.

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Confirm recognition/gamification scope.
2. Define measures and rules.
3. Configure applicable capabilities.
4. Validate outcomes and controls.
5. Establish governance.
