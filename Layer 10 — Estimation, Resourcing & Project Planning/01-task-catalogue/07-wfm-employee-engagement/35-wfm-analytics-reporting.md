## Capability Objective

Provide workforce reporting and analytics required to operate and optimise WFM.

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Define WFM reporting requirements.
2. Define KPIs.
3. Configure reports and views.
4. Validate reporting.
5. Establish operational reporting governance.
