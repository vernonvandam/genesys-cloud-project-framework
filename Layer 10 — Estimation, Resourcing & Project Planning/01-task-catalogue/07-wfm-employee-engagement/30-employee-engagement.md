## Capability Objective

Design employee engagement capabilities that improve workforce experience, participation and operational outcomes.

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Assess engagement requirements.
2. Define engagement objectives.
3. Configure applicable capabilities.
4. Validate employee experience.
5. Establish measurement.
