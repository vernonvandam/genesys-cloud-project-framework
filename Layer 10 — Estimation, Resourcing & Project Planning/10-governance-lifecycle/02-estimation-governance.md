## Capability Objective

Govern how project estimates are created, reviewed, approved, baselined and maintained.

## Layer 1 Mapping

| Phase | Application |
|---|---|
| P03 | Define estimation requirements |
| P04 | Establish estimation controls |
| P09 | Confirm estimate readiness |
| P12 | Capture final estimation outcomes |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Define estimation governance.
2. Establish estimation controls.
3. Review estimate.
4. Approve estimate.
5. Maintain estimation records.
