## Capability Reference

| Attribute | Value |

## Capability Objective

Formally close the project, reconcile final outcomes and preserve lessons and estimation data.

## Layer 1 Mapping

| Phase | Application |
|---|---|
| P12 | Complete project closure |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Confirm completion.
2. Capture final actuals.
3. Analyse variance.
4. Capture lessons.
5. Archive project records.
6. Obtain closure approval.
