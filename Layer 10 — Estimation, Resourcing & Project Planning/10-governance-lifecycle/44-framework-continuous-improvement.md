## Capability Reference

|---|---|

## Capability Objective

Continuously improve the deployment methodology using evidence from projects, platform evolution, customer feedback and estimation outcomes.

## Layer 1 Mapping

| Phase | Application |
|---|---|
| P11 | Identify improvement opportunities |
| P12 | Review, approve and implement framework improvements |

## Source Implementation Activities

1. Collect improvement evidence.
2. Identify improvement opportunities.
3. Assess impact.
4. Prioritise changes.
5. Implement improvements.
6. Validate improvements.
7. Release updated methodology.

# SECTION 10 COMPLETION CRITERIA

Layer 10 — Governance & Lifecycle is complete when the framework can govern the complete lifecycle of the estimation and project-planning model.

```text
Framework Governance
        ↓
Task Catalogue Governance
        ↓
Estimation Governance
        ↓
Role & Resource Governance
        ↓
Dependency Governance
        ↓
Schedule Governance
        ↓
Spreadsheet Governance
        ↓
Baseline Approval
        ↓
Project Delivery Governance
        ↓
Performance Monitoring
        ↓
Reforecasting
        ↓
Actuals & Variance
        ↓
Project Closure
        ↓
Lessons Learned
        ↓
Calibration
        ↓
Methodology Improvement
        ↓
Controlled Framework Release
```

The resulting governance model must ensure that the framework remains:

- controlled
- repeatable
- traceable
- auditable
- estimable
- schedulable
- maintainable
- measurable
- continuously improvable
