# Business Analyst

## Role ID

```text
BA
```

## Role Name

Business Analyst

## Purpose

The Business Analyst translates business objectives, operational processes and stakeholder requirements into structured requirements that can be consumed by the Genesys Cloud solution and implementation teams.

---

# 1. Primary Responsibilities

The Business Analyst is responsible for:

- stakeholder discovery
- requirements elicitation
- business process analysis
- current-state analysis
- future-state analysis
- requirements documentation
- business rules
- process mapping
- acceptance criteria
- stakeholder validation
- requirements traceability
- business readiness support

---

# 2. Typical Layer 1 Activities

- P01 — Mobilisation and Discovery

# 7. Estimation Considerations

Effort is influenced by:

- number of stakeholder groups
- number of business processes
- complexity of requirements
- number of business units
- current-state documentation quality
- number of workshops
- requirement volatility

---

# 8. Definition of Done

The role is considered fulfilled for a task when:

- requirements are documented
- business rules are understood
- stakeholders have reviewed the outcome
- requirements are traceable
- acceptance criteria are defined
- required design inputs have been provided
