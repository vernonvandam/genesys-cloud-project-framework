# 09.08 — Customer Effort Summary

## Purpose

Separate customer responsibilities from delivery-provider effort.

## Required Categories

- Customer SME
- Customer IT
- Customer Security
- Customer Network / Telephony
- Customer Data
- Customer Testing
- Customer Training
- Customer Operations
- Customer Governance

## Required Metrics

- customer task count;
- customer hours;
- delivery hours;
- joint hours;
- peak customer demand.

## Acceptance Criteria

Customer responsibilities are directly traceable to task records.
