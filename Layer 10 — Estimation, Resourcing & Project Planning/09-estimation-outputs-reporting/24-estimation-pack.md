# 09.24 — Estimation Pack

## Purpose

Define the standard package of outputs presented for estimate review and approval.

## Minimum Contents

1. Executive summary
2. Scope summary
3. Project estimate
4. Effort summary
5. Resource summary
6. Schedule
7. Milestones
8. Critical path
9. Customer effort
10. Assumptions
11. Risks
12. Dependencies
13. Estimation confidence
14. Scenario analysis
15. Deliverable summary
16. Approval record

## Approval

The estimation pack shall identify:

- version;
- date;
- author;
- reviewer;
- approver;
- approval status.

## Acceptance Criteria

The pack reconciles to the master estimation workbook and is suitable for formal project approval.
