# 09.17 — Estimation Confidence

## Purpose

Define confidence in the calculated estimate.

## Confidence Drivers

- requirements maturity;
- discovery completeness;
- task completeness;
- historical calibration;
- solution complexity;
- dependencies;
- unknowns;
- customer readiness;
- migration complexity;
- integration complexity.

## Suggested Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Significant unknowns remain |
| Medium | Core scope understood; some uncertainty |
| High | Scope, dependencies and delivery approach well established |

## Acceptance Criteria

Confidence is explicitly stated and supported by documented assumptions and uncertainty.
