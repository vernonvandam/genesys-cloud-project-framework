# 09.22 — Customer Reporting

## Purpose

Define customer-facing reporting derived from the master project model.

## Customer Reporting Should Include

- scope;
- responsibilities;
- schedule;
- milestones;
- customer effort;
- dependencies;
- assumptions;
- risks;
- decisions;
- acceptance activities.

## Customer Data Boundary

Internal commercial information shall not be exposed unless explicitly approved.

## Acceptance Criteria

Customer reporting is consistent with the approved project baseline.
