# 09.20 — Management Reporting

## Purpose

Define executive-level reporting for project estimation and delivery planning.

## Management View

Management reporting should focus on:

- total effort;
- delivery duration;
- major workstreams;
- resource demand;
- major dependencies;
- critical path;
- major assumptions;
- major risks;
- confidence;
- key milestones.

## Principle

Management reporting shall summarise rather than replace the detailed project model.

## Acceptance Criteria

Management reports reconcile to the approved project estimate.
