# 09.03 — Effort Summary

## Purpose

Provide a consolidated view of project effort.

## Required Dimensions

Effort shall be reportable by:

- task;
- capability;
- domain;
- phase;
- workstream;
- role;
- environment;
- customer/delivery responsibility.

## Minimum Metrics

- task count;
- baseline hours;
- adjusted hours;
- contingency;
- total hours;
- percentage of total.

## Acceptance Criteria

Totals reconcile with the master estimate and no task is omitted without an explicit exclusion status.
