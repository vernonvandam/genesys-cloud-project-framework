# Layer 2.07.04 — Business Units

## Purpose

Define the business-unit structure used for workforce planning.

## Classification

**Required**

## Activities

- Identify organisational structure.
- Identify geographic structure.
- Identify operational structure.
- Determine business-unit boundaries.
- Map queues.
- Map planning groups.
- Map management units.
- Validate reporting requirements.
- Configure business units.
- Validate configuration.

## Acceptance Criteria

Business units accurately represent the approved operational model.

## Definition of Done

Business-unit configuration is approved.
