# Layer 2.07.32 — Recognition & Gamification

## Purpose

Support employee recognition and motivation through supported engagement capabilities.

## Classification

**Conditional**

## Activities

- Determine business requirement.
- Define recognition objectives.
- Define eligible employees.
- Define measurement criteria.
- Define governance.
- Configure supported capabilities.
- Test.
- Establish monitoring.

## Acceptance Criteria

Recognition mechanisms align with approved employee engagement objectives.

## Definition of Done

Recognition/gamification is operational or formally excluded.
