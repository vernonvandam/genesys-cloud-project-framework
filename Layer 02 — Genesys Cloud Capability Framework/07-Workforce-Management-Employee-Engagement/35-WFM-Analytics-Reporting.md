# Layer 2.07.35 — WFM Analytics & Reporting

## Purpose

Provide reporting for workforce planning and operational management.

## Metrics

- Forecast accuracy
- Forecast variance
- Staffing requirement
- Actual staffing
- Service level
- Occupancy
- Schedule adherence
- Intraday variance
- Shrinkage
- Time-off
- Overtime
- Understaffing
- Overstaffing
- Employee experience

## Activities

- Define KPI catalogue.
- Define source data.
- Define calculation rules.
- Define dashboards.
- Define report recipients.
- Validate calculations.
- Establish reporting cadence.

## Acceptance Criteria

Required WFM KPIs are measurable and accurate.

## Definition of Done

WFM reporting is operational.
