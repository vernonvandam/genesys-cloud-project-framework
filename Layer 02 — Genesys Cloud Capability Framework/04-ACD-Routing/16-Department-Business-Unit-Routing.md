# Layer 2.04.16 — Department & Business Unit Routing

## 1. Purpose

Define routing based on business organisation, department, product or business unit.

## 2. Classification

**Conditional**

## 3. Activities

- Identify business units.
- Map queues.
- Map users.
- Define division implications.
- Define routing rules.
- Configure routing.
- Test cross-business scenarios.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

Interactions reach the correct operational business unit.

## 5. Definition of Done

Department/business-unit routing is implemented and tested.
