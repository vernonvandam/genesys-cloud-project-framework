# Layer 2.04.20 — Business Hours & Schedule Routing

## 1. Purpose

Ensure routing behaviour changes appropriately according to business schedules.

## 2. Classification

**Required**

## 3. Scope

- Open
- Closed
- Pre-open
- Post-close
- Extended hours
- Time zones
- Regional schedules

## 4. Activities

- Inventory schedules.
- Define time zones.
- Configure schedules.
- Map schedules to routing.
- Test open state.
- Test closed state.
- Test boundary conditions.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

Routing follows approved business schedules.

## 6. Definition of Done

Schedule-based routing is configured and tested.
