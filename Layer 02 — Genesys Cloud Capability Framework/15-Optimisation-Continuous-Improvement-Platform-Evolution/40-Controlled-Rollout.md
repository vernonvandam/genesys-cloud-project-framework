# Layer 2.15.40 — Controlled Rollout

## Capability Definition

Provides controlled and measurable deployment of optimisation or new capability changes across defined populations, business units, queues, channels or environments.

---

# 1. Scope

- Phased rollout
- Deployment waves
- Rollback
- Monitoring
- Success criteria
- Risk management
- Change control

# 2. Classification

**Conditional**

- Identify rollback requirements.
