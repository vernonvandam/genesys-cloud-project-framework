# Layer 2.14.11 — Major Incident Management

## Capability Definition

Major Incident Management defines the enhanced response process for critical Genesys Cloud outages or severe service degradation.

---

# 1. Scope

- Major incident declaration
- Incident command
- Technical bridge
- Business communications
- Vendor escalation
- Executive escalation
- Recovery
- Post-incident review

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify major incident criteria.
- Identify incident commander.
- Identify technical responders.
- Identify executive stakeholders.
- Identify communication channels.
- Identify vendor escalation paths.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define major incident criteria.
- Define command structure.
- Define communication cadence.
- Define technical bridge process.
- Define escalation procedures.
- Define recovery and closure process.
- Define post-incident review.
