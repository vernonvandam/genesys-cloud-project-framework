# Layer 2.14.46 — Licensing Management

## Capability Definition

Licensing Management governs Genesys Cloud licences, feature entitlements, consumption, allocation and ongoing optimisation.

---

# 1. Scope

- Licence inventory
- Licence assignment
- Feature entitlements
- Consumption
- Licence optimisation
- Renewal
- Forecasting
- Governance

# 2. Classification

**Required**

- Identify consumption.
- Identify contract terms.
- Identify renewal dates.
