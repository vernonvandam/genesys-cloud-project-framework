# Layer 2.14.41 — Business Continuity

## Capability Definition

Business Continuity defines how critical contact centre operations continue during significant service disruption.

---

# 1. Scope

- Business continuity
- Critical processes
- Manual workarounds
- Alternative channels
- Alternative routing
- Communications
- Recovery priorities
- Business impact

---

# 2. Classification

**Required**

---

# 3. Discovery Activities

- Identify critical business services.
- Identify continuity requirements.
- Identify dependencies.
- Identify manual workarounds.
- Identify business impact.

---

# 4. Design Activities

- Define continuity objectives.
- Define continuity scenarios.
- Define workaround procedures.
- Define communication plans.
- Define recovery priorities.
- Define business continuity testing.
