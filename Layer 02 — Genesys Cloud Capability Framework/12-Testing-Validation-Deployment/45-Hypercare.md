# 45 — Hypercare

## Capability Definition

Provides enhanced support, monitoring, defect resolution and business validation during the initial post-production stabilisation period.

---

# 1. Scope

- Enhanced support
- Monitoring
- Defect triage
- Business support
- Performance
- Incident management
- Daily reporting
- Stabilisation
