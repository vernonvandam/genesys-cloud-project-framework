# Layer 2.03.22 — Emergency Services / E911

## 1. Purpose

Provide compliant emergency calling capability where required.

## 2. Classification

**Conditional**

Requirements depend heavily on country, region and regulatory framework.

## 3. Discovery

Determine:

- Applicable emergency number(s)
- Regulatory requirements
- Caller location requirements
- Site/location mapping
- Carrier support
- Emergency routing
- Notification requirements
- Testing restrictions
- Operational process

## 4. Activities

- Determine applicable requirements.
- Confirm carrier support.
- Define location mapping.
- Configure emergency calling.
- Document emergency process.
- Perform approved testing.
- Validate expected location information.
- Document limitations.

## 5. Risks

**Critical**

Emergency services must not be assumed to work merely because normal outbound calling works.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

Emergency calling meets applicable customer and regulatory requirements.

## 7. Definition of Done

Emergency calling is configured, validated and operationally documented.
