# Layer 2.06.29 — Digital Recording & Compliance

## Purpose

Apply applicable recording, retention and compliance requirements to digital interactions.

## Classification

**Conditional**

## Scope

- Interaction records
- Retention
- Privacy
- Sensitive data
- Regulatory requirements
- Access control
- Audit

## Activities

- Identify regulatory requirements.
- Define retention.
- Define access.
- Define sensitive-data controls.
- Validate storage.
- Test authorised access.
- Test unauthorised access.

## Acceptance Criteria

Digital interactions comply with approved regulatory and privacy requirements.

## Definition of Done

Compliance controls are validated and approved.
