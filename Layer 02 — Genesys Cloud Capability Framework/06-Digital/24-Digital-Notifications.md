# Layer 2.06.24 — Digital Notifications

## Purpose

Provide customer notifications associated with digital interactions.

## Classification

**Conditional**

## Potential Uses

- Conversation acknowledgement
- Queue notifications
- Callback
- Email acknowledgement
- Status notifications
- Follow-up messages

## Activities

- Identify notification use cases.
- Define trigger.
- Define message.
- Define recipient.
- Define opt-in/opt-out.
- Configure.
- Test.

## Acceptance Criteria

Notifications are delivered only under approved conditions.

## Definition of Done

Required notifications are operational.
