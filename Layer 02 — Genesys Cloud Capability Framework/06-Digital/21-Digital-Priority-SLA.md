# Layer 2.06.21 — Digital Priority & SLA

## Purpose

Define service levels appropriate to each digital channel.

## Classification

**Required**

## Considerations

- Synchronous vs asynchronous interaction
- Email response time
- Messaging response time
- Priority customers
- Regulatory requirements
- Business hours
- Queue ageing
- Escalation

## Activities

- Define service-level targets.
- Define priority rules.
- Define business hours.
- Define escalation.
- Configure priority.
- Configure SLA reporting.
- Test priority behaviour.

## Acceptance Criteria

Digital service levels reflect approved business requirements.

## Definition of Done

Digital SLA design is approved and measurable.
