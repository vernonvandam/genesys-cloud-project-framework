# Layer 2.06.28 — Digital Analytics & Reporting

## Purpose

Measure digital channel performance and customer outcomes.

## Classification

**Required**

## Metrics

- Interaction volume
- Response time
- Service level
- Abandonment
- Queue time
- Resolution
- Transfer
- Escalation
- Bot containment
- Bot failure
- Email response time
- Agent handling
- Customer satisfaction
- Channel utilisation

## Activities

- Define KPI catalogue.
- Define reporting requirements.
- Map source data.
- Validate metrics.
- Create dashboards.
- Validate calculations.
- Train users.

## Acceptance Criteria

Required digital KPIs can be measured accurately.

## Definition of Done

Digital reporting is available to operational stakeholders.
