# Layer 2.06.12 — Email

## Purpose

Provide customer service through email interactions.

## Classification

**Required**

## Scope

- Email addresses
- Inbound email
- Outbound email
- Queue
- Routing
- Auto-response
- Attachments
- SLA
- Templates
- Customer context

## Activities

- Inventory service email addresses.
- Define email queue model.
- Define routing.
- Define auto-response.
- Define operating hours.
- Define SLA.
- Configure email.
- Configure agent handling.
- Test inbound.
- Test outbound.
- Test attachments.
- Test customer context.

## Acceptance Criteria

Inbound and outbound email operate correctly and route to the intended service teams.

## Definition of Done

Email channels are production-ready.
